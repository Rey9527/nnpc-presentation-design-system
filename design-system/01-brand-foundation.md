# 01 — Brand Foundation

## Positioning

NNPC is an enterprise AI and digital transformation partner that connects technology, business requirements, and cross-border execution.

NNPC should not visually present itself as a hardware reseller, generic system integrator, or decorative technology brand. The presentation system must communicate strategic judgment, implementation capability, and technical credibility.

## Brand Promise

**Between Nations, Beyond Systems.**

This represents the ability to connect:

- countries and cultures;
- business and technology;
- software, hardware, data, and operations;
- strategy and implementation;
- individual systems into an integrated enterprise capability.

## Brand Personality

- Professional
- Premium
- International
- Technical
- Trustworthy
- Decisive
- Practical

## Style Formula

### Apple — 40%

Use restraint, large whitespace, strong image composition, simple typography, and one dominant message per slide.

### McKinsey — 40%

Use clear hierarchy, business logic, structured evidence, comparison, implication, ROI, and action-oriented storytelling.

### NVIDIA — 20%

Use enterprise AI, computing infrastructure, high-performance systems, and technical confidence. Avoid imitation of NVIDIA green or gaming aesthetics.

## Desired Audience Impression

The audience should think:

> NNPC understands the business problem, can design the right architecture, and can make the solution work in the real operating environment.

## Core Design Principle

Every slide should communicate one idea with maximum clarity.

## Content Logic

Preferred narrative sequence:

1. Business context
2. Problem or opportunity
3. Insight
4. Recommended solution
5. Architecture or implementation
6. Business value and ROI
7. Next action
