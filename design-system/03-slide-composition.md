# 03 — Slide Composition

## Output Format

- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Primary use: full-slide image or visual background in Google Slides
- Final text: added manually in Google Slides
- Generated visuals should therefore preserve intentional text-safe areas.

## Composition Principles

1. One primary idea per slide.
2. One dominant visual hierarchy.
3. Use whitespace deliberately.
4. Prefer a few large elements over many small elements.
5. Every decorative element must support meaning, direction, or brand recognition.
6. Leave enough clear space for editable Google Slides text.

## Text-Safe Areas

Unless the requested layout specifies otherwise:

- Keep the left or right 35–45% visually quiet for title and body text.
- Avoid placing critical visual details near slide edges.
- Keep the bottom 5% clear for optional page numbers or source notes.
- Do not generate fake body copy, charts with unreadable labels, or random interface text.

## Light Mode

Use for most content pages:

- white or warm-white background;
- black typography area;
- neutral gray structure;
- restrained yellow accents;
- premium documentary or architectural imagery.

## Dark Mode

Use selectively for:

- cover pages;
- section dividers;
- major AI infrastructure statements;
- exhibition or keynote moments;
- final pages.

Dark slides should use near-black rather than pure black where tonal depth is helpful. Yellow and white must remain controlled.

## Preferred Layout Families

### Hero Split

One large image area and one clean text-safe area. Suitable for covers, strategic statements, and solution introductions.

### Architectural Grid

Structured modules connected by fine rules. Suitable for capabilities, portfolios, or system layers.

### Central System

A central product, platform, or architecture with restrained connections. Suitable for AI workstation, RTLS, IoT, and integration topics.

### Journey

A clear directional path with three to five stages. Suitable for transformation roadmaps and implementation plans.

### Evidence Focus

One key metric or chart area dominates. Supporting visual elements remain secondary.

### Comparison

Two or three clearly separated alternatives. Do not create dense feature matrices as part of the image; leave structured zones for editable text.

## Density Rules

- Default: low to medium visual density.
- Maximum of three major visual groups.
- Avoid dashboard-like clutter unless the slide specifically represents an operational dashboard.
- Do not fill empty areas merely because space is available.

## Image Treatment

- Prefer realistic enterprise environments.
- Use controlled contrast and neutral color grading.
- Integrate yellow through physical details, light, lines, or selected objects—not through global yellow tinting.
- Human subjects should look credible, purposeful, and naturally placed.
- Technology should look implementable, not imaginary.
