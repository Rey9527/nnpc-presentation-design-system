# NNPC Presentation Visual Preflight Checklist

Use this checklist before approving any generated slide image.

## Brand

- Does the image use black, white, neutral gray, and restrained NNPC Yellow?
- Is yellow used to guide attention rather than dominate the slide?
- Does the result feel like NNPC rather than a generic technology company?
- Does the visual communicate professionalism, technical credibility, and implementation capability?

## Composition

- Is the output 16:9?
- Is there one clearly dominant idea?
- Is there enough whitespace?
- Is a clear text-safe area preserved for Google Slides?
- Are the major objects aligned intentionally?
- Are there no more than three major visual groups?

## Image Quality

- Is the subject realistic and commercially credible?
- Are people, machinery, servers, screens, and architecture physically plausible?
- Is the image free from malformed hands, duplicated objects, broken equipment, or impossible interfaces?
- Is the resolution sufficient for full-slide use?

## Prohibited Elements

- No generic blue technology background
- No cyberpunk or neon treatment
- No glowing hologram above a hand
- No excessive hexagons or circuit patterns
- No gaming aesthetic
- No random text, fake logos, or illegible labels
- No unnecessary gradients or decorative clutter

## Google Slides Readiness

- Can final text be added without covering the main subject?
- Is the background behind the future text sufficiently quiet?
- Are the slide edges and footer area clear?
- Will the image remain legible when projected in a meeting room?

## Approval Rule

Reject and regenerate the visual when any major defect affects brand consistency, physical plausibility, text placement, or presentation readability.
